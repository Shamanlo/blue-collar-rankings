4 things to know before you start:
1. Layout matters more than anything. A poor layout can make even a brand-new kitchen frustrating to use.
2. Moving plumbing increases cost. Keeping the sink and dishwasher where they are controls your budget.
3. Lighting affects function and appearance. Under-cabinet, task and overhead lighting all play a role.
4. Material choices drive your budget: cabinets, countertops, flooring and finishes.

2026 kitchen remodel prices in Santa Clarita:
• Basic kitchen remodel: $45,000 – $55,000+ (new paint, flooring, countertops and lighting with more affordable materials)
• In-depth kitchen makeover: $55,000 – $75,000+ (most popular: design and layout changes, higher-end materials, new cabinets and countertops, flooring, lighting and paint)
• Luxury kitchen: $75,000 – $150,000+ (premium materials, custom everything, top-tier appliances)

What a full-service contractor handles: layout and kitchen design, open-concept kitchens, quartz and granite countertops, new and custom cabinets, new flooring, kitchen islands and seating, storage, electrical and plumbing upgrades, tile backsplashes and under-cabinet lighting.

Common questions:
• How long does it take? A few weeks for a minor remodel; a few months for a full renovation.
• Do I need a permit? Most major kitchen remodels do, and your contractor should pull it.
• Can I live at home during the remodel? Yes, often with a temporary setup for water and appliances.
• How can I save? Choose budget-friendly materials and keep your layout.

How to pick a kitchen contractor: a California license you can verify, bonded and insured, pulls permits and handles code compliance, written estimates and clear timelines, handles both design and build, and knows Santa Clarita's building department. Settle layout changes early: changing the layout mid-project means change orders and delays.
