What is AEO?
Answer Engine Optimization is the work of making your business easy for AI assistants to understand, trust and recommend, so when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude or Google AI Overviews for a local business, yours is in the answer. You'll also hear it called GEO, AIO, AI SEO or LLM SEO.

AEO vs. SEO:
• SEO: rank in a list of ten blue links (keywords, backlinks, page speed)
• AEO: get named in the answer itself (consistent facts, specific reviews, clear answers, trusted mentions)
• People now ask full questions: "Who can fix my water heater in Ankeny today?"
• You still need SEO. The two work together.

Why it matters for blue-collar businesses:
When a pipe bursts, a furnace quits or hail hits a roof, people ask AI who can come today. AI names only two or three businesses. If yours isn't one of them, you don't get the call.

The 5 signals AI looks for:
1. Consistent listings on Google, Bing, Apple Maps and Yelp
2. Specific reviews that mention the job and the town
3. An answer-ready website
4. Structured data (schema) for your services, service area and hours
5. Trusted mentions in local press, guides and directories

What tips the answer for contractors: a service-area list naming every town you cover, emergency and after-hours availability stated the same way everywhere, reviews that mention the job and town, and licensing, insurance and warranty details AI can quote. Your Google Business Profile still matters a lot.
