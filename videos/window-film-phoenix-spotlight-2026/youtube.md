Phoenix sits in the Sonoran Desert at a latitude where the summer sun climbs nearly overhead at midday. Roof overhangs shade much of the south glass in July, but east windows take hard, low sun every morning and west windows take it from mid-afternoon until dark.

Window film can block up to 99% of UV rays, up to 80% of solar heat and up to 90% of glare (typical upper ranges; your numbers depend on the film and the glass).

Window film for Phoenix homes:
• UV Protection: Stops almost all UV at the pane
• Privacy: Daytime privacy for bathrooms
• Decorative: Etched, striped and patterned film for front-door sidelights
• Security: Thick clear safety film for sliders, sidelights and ground-floor windows

Commercial window film:
• Anti-Graffiti: A clear sacrificial skin on storefront, lobby and light-rail-facing glass
• Decorative & Frosted Glass: Etched-look strips, plotter-cut company marks
• Solar Control: Reduces solar heat on big commercial glass
• Conference Room Privacy: Frosted or graduated bands on glass meeting rooms that hide screens

Window Tinting for Phoenix Homes: Valley houses span a century of styles. Central Phoenix historic districts such as Willo, Encanto-Palmcroft and Roosevelt hold 1920s bungalows and Spanish Colonial Revival homes, often with original single-pane wood windows.

How an install works: send rough window sizes through the quote form, see film samples on your own glass, installation day (a typical house in a day or less), then a short curing period.

Common questions:
• Do we need landlord or HOA approval for window film? Usually.
• What lifespan should I expect from film under Arizona sun? Film applied to the inside of the glass usually stays in service for many years; the manufacturer's written warranty spells out the term.
• Does window film help during monsoon dust storms? Safety film holds glass together if a pane breaks from wind-blown debris, which reduces sharp fragments inside.
