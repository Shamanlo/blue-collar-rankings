### Description

How much does a new roof cost in Iowa City in 2026? A full residential roof replacement in the Iowa City area typically runs $7,840 to $19,600, with most standard-sized homes landing between $9,800 and $14,700. This pricing guide breaks down the price ranges, cost by home size, cost by roof type, how Iowa's hail, wind, ice and humidity affect your roof, storm damage and insurance, and the extra costs to plan for.

Iowa roof cost by type (median-sized home):
• Asphalt shingles: $4.65/sq ft, about $4,794. Most popular and most affordable; lasts about 20–25 years
• Metal: $8.64/sq ft, about $8,911. Handles hail, wind and heavy snow; lasts 40+ years
• Wood shingles & shakes: $11.06/sq ft, about $11,401. Classic look, but humid summers can cause rot and mold
• Clay tile: $15.23/sq ft, about $15,703. Long-lasting, heavy, needs a specialist
• Slate: $15.30/sq ft, about $15,779. Top-of-the-line and the priciest
Class 4 impact-resistant asphalt shingles are rated for hail up to 2 inches and can qualify for insurance premium discounts.

How Iowa weather hits your roof:
• Spring hail: cracks shingles. Class 4 shingles hold up and can lower premiums
• Straight-line winds: lift shingles. Metal and wind-rated shingles hold on
• Ice dams: back water up under the eaves. Ice-and-water shield along eaves and valleys stops it
• Heavy snow: adds weight. Metal sheds snow and handles the load
• Freeze-thaw cycles: damage roof decking, which can add repair costs
• Humid summers: promote rot and mold. Good ventilation is a must

Storm damage: homeowners insurance often covers hail and wind damage, minus your deductible. Get the damage documented with photos and have your roofer meet the adjuster on-site.

Other costs to plan for: tear-off of the old roof (about 1.5 hours of labor per 100 sq ft), permits (most Iowa cities require one), rotted decking, and hidden damage (about 14% of homeowners said their final bill came in higher than their estimate).

Timing: the best time to reroof in Iowa is May through September. The average Iowa roof lasts 20–30 years. Iowa roofing prices run about 1–2% lower than Illinois and Wisconsin and about 12% lower than Minnesota.
