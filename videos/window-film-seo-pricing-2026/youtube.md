How much does SEO and marketing cost for a window tinting or window film company in 2026? Most installers pay somewhere between $300 and $5,000 a month. This pricing guide breaks down the four tiers agencies charge, what you get at each level, how it compares to SEO pricing across all industries, what moves your price, and six questions to ask before you sign.

2026 window film SEO & marketing prices (per month):
• Starter: $300 – $500 (Google Business Profile management: categories, services, photos and posts; some on-site optimization)
• Growth: $500 – $1,000 (everything in Starter, plus citations on Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing and directories; analytics reporting; some backlinks)
• Multi-city: $1,000 – $2,000 (a page for every film type and service city; steady citations and backlinks; a review system that names the film; monthly reports on calls and estimate requests)
• Competitive market: $2,000 – $5,000 (for big, crowded markets: aggressive backlink building, citations everywhere buyers look, custom reporting by booked job, lead generation)

How that compares across all industries (SEO.com):
• Local SEO: $500 – $2,500 a month
• Traditional SEO: $1,500 – $5,000 a month
• Hourly SEO work: $75 – $200 an hour
• One-time projects: $1,000 – $30,000

What moves your price: your market (a big metro costs more than a small town), how many cities you serve, which film types you sell (residential, commercial, security, decorative, auto), your website's health, AI search visibility (often its own line item), and video and social media.

Is it worth it? One commercial window film lead can be a $20,000+ job. The top 3 on Google Maps get the calls. Profile fixes can help within weeks; rankings usually build over 3 to 6 months.

6 questions to ask before you sign:
1. Does it cover AI search like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews?
2. Is software billed on top of the agency fee?
3. Do reports show calls and booked jobs, or just rankings?
4. Is it month-to-month?
5. Do I own my website and listings?
6. Do you work with my competitors?
