2026 Malibu kitchen remodel prices (depending on size and scope):
• Basic kitchen remodel: $45K – $55K+
• In-depth kitchen makeover (most popular): $55K – $75K+
• Luxury kitchen: $75K – $150K+

What each tier includes:
• Basic: new paint, flooring, countertops and lighting; existing layout; refaced or new stock cabinets; new backsplash
• In-depth: layout changes, new semi-custom cabinets, quartz or granite counters, tile backsplash, appliance upgrades, plumbing or electrical relocations
• Luxury: layout redesign and wall changes, custom cabinetry, full-slab quartz or granite, commercial-grade appliances, custom island or built-in seating

What affects your price: size and layout, cabinets, countertops, appliances, plumbing and electrical, flooring and lighting.

Malibu considerations: salt air (marine-grade hardware and corrosion-resistant fixtures), California Coastal Commission review for exterior or footprint changes, and layouts that frame ocean views.

Estimated permit costs:
• Minor remodel, no layout changes: $420 – $735
• Wall removal, plumbing or electrical upgrades: $840 – $1,260
• Major remodel with structural changes: $1,260 – $1,575+
(Based on 2026 Southern California permit averages. Confirm with the City of Malibu.)

Optional upgrades that add cost: panel-ready appliances, custom storage and pantry pull-outs, slab backsplashes, heated floors, a beverage or coffee bar, and new windows, doors or skylights.
