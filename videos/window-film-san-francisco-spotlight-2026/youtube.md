San Francisco Window Film Pros install window film on San Francisco flats, row houses, storefronts and office floors. What your glass needs depends a lot on which side of the hills you live on, which way the windows face, and how close they sit to the sidewalk.

San Francisco is a small city with very different weather from one ridge to the next. On a typical July afternoon the Outer Sunset and the Richmond District can sit under a grey marine layer while the Mission, Potrero Hill and Dogpatch are in full sun.

Window film can block up to 99% of UV rays, up to 80% of solar heat and up to 90% of glare (typical upper ranges; your numbers depend on the film and the glass).

Window film for San Francisco homes:
• Heat Control: Film that rejects solar heat on the south
• UV Protection: Nearly clear film that blocks almost all ultraviolet light to slow fading
• Privacy: Frosted, one-way and gradient films for lightwell windows
• Decorative: Etched, patterned and stained-glass-look films for entry doors

Commercial window film:
• Commercial Security: Security film, with optional attachment systems
• Anti-Graffiti: Clear sacrificial film that takes tags
• Decorative & Frosted Glass: Frosted, etched-look and printed films for office partitions
• Solar Control: Solar control film for commercial curtain walls and large glazing

San Francisco Rules That Touch Window Film Projects: Interior window film is usually not a permit item, but a few local rules still come up. Buildings in historic districts and designated landmarks go through Planning review for visible exterior changes, so a mirrored film that changes how a facade reads from the street deserves a check first.

How an install works: send rough window sizes through the quote form, see film samples on your own glass, installation day (a typical house in a day or less), then a short curing period.

Common questions:
• Do I need window film if I live in the foggy part of the city? Often not for heat.
• Is tinted glass going to make my SF flat gloomy? Only if you pick a dark product.
• Can you put film on old Victorian glass? Yes, in most cases.
