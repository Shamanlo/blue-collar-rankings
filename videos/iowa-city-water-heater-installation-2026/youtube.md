How much does it cost to install a new water heater in Iowa City in 2026? Most installs run from about $950 to $6,000. This pricing guide breaks down the cost of every system type, unit vs. labor, tank vs. tankless, what moves your price, and the signs it's time to replace.

2026 average total installed cost (unit + labor):
• Electric tankless: $1,100 – $2,700
• Electric tank: $1,200 – $3,000
• Gas tank: $1,300 – $4,050
• Heat pump / hybrid: $2,000 – $6,300
• Gas tankless: $3,300 – $6,250

Unit vs. labor:
• Units run about $700 – $4,800
• Labor runs about $300 – $3,500
• Gas tankless costs the most to install: it usually needs a bigger gas line and different venting

Tank or tankless?
• Tank: lower upfront cost, usually a straight swap, hot water limited to what's in the tank
• Tankless: higher upfront cost, continuous hot water, mounts on the wall, often needs gas line and venting changes

What moves your price: the type and size of the unit, gas line upgrades, venting changes, and tight utility spaces.

Signs it's time to replace: water leaking from the bottom, rusty or discolored hot water, popping or rumbling sounds, hot water running out faster, or a tank over 10 years old. Most tanks last about 8 to 12 years. A leaking tank is an emergency.

Iowa tip: Eastern Iowa water is hard. Flush your tank now and then to slow scale buildup, and descale tankless units periodically. Ask your utility about rebates on efficient models.
