2026 bathroom remodel prices in Westlake Village:
• Basic bathroom update: $5,250 – $15,750 (new vanity, toilet, faucet & lights; simple tile or vinyl floors; no major layout or plumbing changes)
• Mid-range remodel: $15,750 – $26,250 (tub-to-shower conversion; new tile throughout; upgraded vanity with stone countertop; minor layout changes)
• High-end remodel: $31,500+ (full master bath; custom walk-in shower or freestanding tub; double vanity; built-in storage; radiant heated floors)

Cost by type of bathroom:
• Powder room: $5,250 – $12,600
• Hall bathroom (most common): $15,750 – $26,250
• Master bathroom: $26,250 – $52,500+

What moves your budget: size and layout, scope of work, plumbing and electrical changes (keeping fixtures in place saves the most), materials, permits, and the bathroom's location and access.

Westlake Village notes:
• Permits: roughly $525 – $2,100; fees scale with your project's value
• HOAs: Westlake Village has twenty neighborhoods with active HOAs. Interior remodels usually don't need approval, but structural or exterior changes may.
