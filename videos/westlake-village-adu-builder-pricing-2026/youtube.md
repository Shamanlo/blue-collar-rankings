2026 ADU prices in Westlake Village (design, permits & construction):
• Junior ADU (up to 500 sq ft, inside the house): $65,000 – $130,000+
• Garage conversion (studio or 1-bedroom): $110,000 – $220,000+
• Attached ADU (new addition to the house): $175,000 – $325,000+
• Detached ADU (new backyard home): $220,000 – $435,000+

ADU examples around Westlake Village:
• First Neighborhood: Garage conversion on an original tract lot ($110,000 – $220,000+). The earliest Westlake streets, with attached garages on level lots.

What drives ADU cost in Westlake Village:
• Which agency permits it: City + LA County, Thousand Oaks or Ventura County
• Oak trees: Protected oaks shape the footprint
• Hillside pads: Structural work instead of cutting into slopes
• Well & septic: Hidden Valley ranch parcels
• HOA review: Colors, materials & roof style
• Finishes: Kitchens & baths concentrate cost

Rules to know: A city can require no more than 4-foot side and rear setbacks. A city has 60 days to act on a complete application. A junior ADU tops out at 500 square feet. And in Westlake Village, you need two approvals in a row: a city Zoning Clearance, then an LA County building permit.

Common questions:
• Which city permits it? Many Westlake addresses are in Thousand Oaks.
• Can my HOA stop it? No, but reasonable design rules apply.
• How close to the line? 4-foot side & rear setbacks under state law.
• Need parking? Not for conversions; max one space otherwise.
