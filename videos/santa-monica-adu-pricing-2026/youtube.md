2026 ADU price ranges in Santa Monica (design/build):
• Junior ADU (JADU): $75K – $150K+
• Garage conversion ADU: $125K – $250K+
• Attached ADU: $200K – $375K+
• Detached ADU: $250K – $500K+

What drives your ADU price:
1. Size & bedrooms: bigger costs more overall, smaller costs more per sq ft
2. New build vs. conversion: reusing a foundation and framing usually costs less
3. Utilities: sewer, water, gas, electrical and panel upgrades
4. Site access: narrow side yards and alley access on Westside lots
5. Coastal zone: Coastal Development Permit review near the beach
6. Finishes: cabinets, counters, flooring and fixtures

Permits & fees:
• ADUs of 750 sq ft or less are exempt from state impact fees
• The City must act on a complete application within 60 days
• Completeness check within 15 business days (since Jan 1, 2026)
• No parking required for ADUs in Santa Monica
