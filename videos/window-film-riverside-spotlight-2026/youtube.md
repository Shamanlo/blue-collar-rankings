Riverside sits far enough from the ocean that the cool coastal air rarely does much for it in summer. Triple-digit afternoons are common from June into September, and warm spells can arrive in May and linger well into October.

Window film can block up to 99% of UV rays, up to 80% of solar heat and up to 90% of glare (typical upper ranges; your numbers depend on the film and the glass).

Window film for Riverside homes:
• Heat Control: Film that rejects solar heat on sliding doors
• UV Protection: Nearly clear film that blocks the ultraviolet behind faded hardwood
• Privacy: Frosted, reflective or patterned film for bathrooms
• Decorative: Etched, frosted and patterned film for entry sidelights

Commercial window film:
• Commercial Security: Safety and security film with optional frame attachment for retail
• Anti-Graffiti: Clear sacrificial film that takes scratches, etching and tags
• Decorative & Frosted Glass: Frosted bands, cut logos and gradient film for office partitions
• Solar Control: High-rejection solar film for large commercial glass

Window Tinting for Riverside's Historic and Newer Homes: Riverside's housing stock covers a wide range. Around downtown and in the Wood Streets you find Craftsman bungalows and Spanish revival houses with original wood sashes and single-pane glass, some of it old and wavy.

How an install works: send rough window sizes through the quote form, see film samples on your own glass, installation day (a typical house in a day or less), then a short curing period.

Common questions:
• Can I put window film on dual-pane windows? Usually yes, with the right film.
• Can film affect the warranty on my windows? Certain window makers reduce their coverage once aftermarket film goes on, and certain film makers back the glass to make up for it.
• Does my HOA allow window tinting? Many HOAs in Orangecrest, Eastvale, Menifee, Murrieta and Temecula regulate how windows look from the street, and some restrict mirrored film.
