### Description

Miami Window Film Pros puts solar, glare, privacy and safety film on business and household glass all over Miami-Dade, from Brickell office floors and Wynwood galleries to Kendall ranch houses. In a city with no real winter, the glass fights the air conditioning twelve months a year.

Most American cities get a break from cooling for part of the year. Miami barely does.

Window film can block up to 99% of UV rays, up to 80% of solar heat and up to 90% of glare (typical upper ranges; your numbers depend on the film and the glass).

Window film for Miami homes:
• Privacy: Frosted, mirrored and gradient finishes for bathrooms
• Decorative: Etched, striped and patterned films for front-door glass
• Security: Heavy clear film that holds a cracked pane together
• Glare Reduction: Takes the edge off reflections on TVs

Commercial window film:
• Decorative & Frosted Glass: Frosted bands, full frost, cut-out logos
• Solar Control: Heat and glare rejection on big commercial glass
• Conference Room Privacy: Frosted or patterned film on glass meeting rooms
• Low-E: For older Miami buildings with single-pane glass

Hurricane Season and Security Window Film in Miami: Hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, and after Hurricane Andrew in 1992 Miami-Dade adopted some of the strictest building requirements in the country. Openings in the county's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone are protected with impact windows or shutters that carry product approval.

How an install works: send rough window sizes through the quote form, see film samples on your own glass, installation day (a typical house in a day or less), then a short curing period.

Common questions:
• Will my condo association allow window film? Most Miami associations accept interior film that leaves the facade looking unchanged, though nearly all want to see it first.
• How long does window film last in Miami sun? Interior film on home and office glass usually lasts many years, and the film maker's warranty states the term.
• How many days does new film need to cure in Miami? Usually several days to a few weeks.
