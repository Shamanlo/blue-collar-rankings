4 things to know before you start

  1. Layout matters more than anything. A poor layout can make even a brand-new kitchen frustrating to use.
  2. Moving plumbing increases cost. Keeping the sink and dishwasher where they are is the easiest way to control your budget.
  3. Lighting affects function and appearance. Under-cabinet, task and overhead lighting all play a role.
  4. Material choices drive your budget: cabinets, countertops, flooring and finishes.

What a full-service contractor handles

  • Layout and kitchen design, including open-concept kitchens
  • New and custom cabinets, quartz and granite countertops
  • Kitchen islands, seating and storage
  • New flooring, tile backsplashes and under-cabinet lighting
  • Electrical and plumbing upgrades

How to pick a kitchen contractor in Santa Clarita

  • A California contractor's license you can verify with the CSLB
  • Bonded and insured
  • Pulls the permits and handles code compliance
  • Written estimates and clear timelines
  • Handles both design and build
  • Knows Santa Clarita's building department

Settle layout changes early. Changing the layout mid-project means change orders and delays.
